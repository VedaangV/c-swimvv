\noindent\textbf{Introduction}

This Supporting Information reports the full per-scenario benefit, cost, and
benefit--cost-ratio values underlying Figure~7 (``Net benefit by scenario for
Islands A, B, and C'') of the main text. The main text summarizes these
results as a single comparative figure; the tables here give the exact
values for readers who wish to use or reproduce specific numbers.
